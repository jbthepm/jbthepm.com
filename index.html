Common questions

Straight answers

What does a digital product consultant actually do?

A digital product consultant helps a business decide what software to build and then gets it built. That covers validating the idea, defining scope, designing the user experience, managing the build, and launching it. JB de la Garza works as a solo consultant, so the person who scopes your project is the person who delivers it.

Who does JB the PM work with?

JB the PM works with founders, small businesses, and teams that have engineering capacity but no dedicated product owner. Typical clients are pre-seed to Series A startups, and established small companies that need an internal tool or a customer-facing web app built properly.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope. Engagements generally start with a paid discovery sprint that produces a fixed-price proposal for the build, so there are no open-ended hourly bills. Fractional product management is billed as a monthly retainer. Book a 30-minute call for a specific number.

What is Answer Search Optimization (ASO)?

Answer Search Optimization is the practice of structuring a website so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can read it and cite it when someone asks a related question. It relies on plain HTML text, clear question-and-answer sections, and structured schema markup, not keyword stuffing. JB the PM builds every site this way by default.

Where is JB de la Garza located?

JB de la Garza is based in Austin, Texas, and works remotely with clients across the United States. Local Austin clients can meet in person.

How long does a typical project take?

A marketing website typically takes two to four weeks. A first working version of a custom web application typically takes six to twelve weeks depending on scope. Discovery sprints run one to two weeks.
